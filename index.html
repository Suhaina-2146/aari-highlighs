Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Modern Luxury

✦

Ari Highlights Boutique is a passion project born from eight years of mastering the ancient art of Aari embroidery. Every blouse, jacket, and creation leaving our studio carries the soul of traditional Tamil craftsmanship elevated to contemporary luxury.

  • Handcrafted by master artisans with 8+ years of expertise
  • Premium fabrics sourced from the finest mills
  • Each piece takes 40–120 hours to complete
  • Custom measurements for a perfect, personal fit
  • Delivered with love, packed with elegance
